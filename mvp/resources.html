How these resources are used

Foundational means the source shapes one or more playbook principles, domains, decisions, safeguards, tools, or learning objectives. Contextual sources help users understand the marketplace or emerging practice but do not independently support a toolkit recommendation. Every agency must still conduct local legal, policy, privacy, security, equity, accessibility, tribal sovereignty, labor, procurement, and community review.

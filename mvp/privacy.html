Foundation Edition privacy notice

Privacy and data boundaries

The preview site is designed to minimize data collection while launch services are being finalized.

Current preview behavior

The forms in this local preview do not transmit or store submissions. They demonstrate the information the launch experience may request after an approved form and email service is connected.

Information that should never be submitted

Do not submit protected health information, personal health information, confidential agency information, credentials, security-sensitive details, personnel records, procurement-sensitive information, or restricted data through any Playbook form.

Planned launch controls

Before public collection begins, the site will identify the service provider, data elements, purpose, retention period, access controls, deletion process, and contact route. Consent for release updates will remain separate from reviewer or feedback participation.

Analytics

Any launch analytics should be privacy-minimizing and limited to understanding qualified visits, content use, tool starts and completions, subscriptions, reviewer interest, and consulting inquiries. Sensitive assessment or health information must not be sent to analytics services.

Questions or corrections

Use the feedback route for privacy questions, corrections, or removal requests after data collection is enabled.

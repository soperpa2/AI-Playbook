How engagements are structured

Offerings use fixed-fee scopes with clear inclusions, exclusions, participants, deliverables, timing, and local responsibilities. Licensing, facilitation, assessment, customization, travel, and advisory support are separated so buyers understand what they are purchasing.

PHI Solutions will not support unsafe deployment, unsupported claims, inappropriate data use, or work that bypasses required public health governance.

Evidence-informed and implementation-focused

Credible sources translated into organizational action.

The playbook integrates peer-reviewed research, authoritative guidance, standards and frameworks, policy materials, workforce resources, and documented public health practice. Evidence type, strength, uncertainty, and local validation needs are kept distinct.

01

Identify

Define the decision, capability, risk, or organizational output addressed by each play.

02

Assess

Consider source authority, rigor, relevance, currency, transparency, and public health transferability.

03

Translate

Convert supported principles into actions, decision checks, tools, and accountable outputs.

04

Trace

Connect every play to its evidence summary, provenance types, key sources, limitations, and review date.

Transparent about what the evidence can support

Evidence for public health AI is uneven and rapidly changing. Some recommendations are supported by research; others appropriately draw on authoritative guidance, standards, ethics, policy, or expert synthesis. All tools require local legal, policy, operational, equity, and community review.

Early reviewer network

Review new tools before they are released.

Help ensure new resources are practical, understandable, evidence-informed, and appropriate for real public health settings. Selected reviewers receive early access to tools aligned with their experience.

What reviewers do

  • Review selected pre-release tools or guidance
  • Test clarity and practical usability
  • Identify missing perspectives, risks, and evidence
  • Suggest improvements before public release

What reviewers receive

  • Early access to selected new tools
  • Release updates and reviewer opportunities
  • Optional acknowledgment, with permission
  • A direct way to shape future resources

Participation is voluntary and assignment depends on topic fit and review capacity. Pre-release materials should not be redistributed.

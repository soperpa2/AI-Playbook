Essentials Edition · Version 0.9

Useful enough to act. Bounded enough to learn.

This Essentials Edition includes the complete 13-play pathway, one available Essentials Tool for each play, core learning, foundational resources, and reviewer and feedback routes.

What is included

  • The PLAN–BUILD–DEPLOY–GOVERN pathway and all 13 play overviews
  • One available Essentials Tool for every play
  • Core all-staff learning and previews of role-based paths
  • Evidence labels, source landscape, interpretation limits, and review dates
  • Reviewer recruitment, feedback, and correction pathways

What is intentionally not included

The public build does not contain the complete editable toolkit, diagnostic weights, scoring and interpretation rules, benchmarks, prioritization logic, facilitation protocols, recommendation methods, organization workspaces, or reporting systems. These completed capabilities are reserved for organizational products and services that are not yet publicly released.

How content is maintained

Each public play and Essentials Tool has a stable content ID, release version, evidence label, and review information. Material corrections are reviewed and reflected in future releases. Evidence is reviewed annually, with interim updates when important guidance or risks change.

Appropriate use

This material supports planning and learning. It does not replace local legal, privacy, security, procurement, accessibility, equity, workforce, Tribal governance, community, or scientific review. Public health organizations remain responsible for decisions and implementation.

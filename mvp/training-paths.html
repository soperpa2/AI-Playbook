Training paths matched to public health responsibilities.

After completing the core all-staff modules, learners will be able to follow role-based and functional paths aligned with the decisions and work they perform.

Shared foundation first

Every path begins with the core learning modules so staff share essential vocabulary, safe-use expectations, and escalation practices.

Assigned by responsibility

Organizations can combine paths based on job duties, governance participation, projects, and access to AI-supported systems.

Applied evidence of learning

Paths will pair knowledge checks with exercises, workplace artifacts, supervisor verification, and refresher learning.

Help shape the curriculum

Review paths and modules before release.

Practitioners with relevant experience can apply for early access and provide feedback on usefulness, accuracy, role fit, and missing perspectives.
